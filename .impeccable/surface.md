# Inbox prototype

Mode: Operate. Static, code-led prototype; no generated imagery.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Challenges are mail. Folders organize categories, and only solving marks mail as read.

OWN-WORLD: Outlook Express in Windows XP: cobalt title chrome, warm gray toolbars, inset white panes, Tahoma text, tiny authored envelope and folder icons. User-pinned direction overrides seed edc69d60.

STORY: Pick a folder, scan subjects and points, read the message, reply with a flag. Unread counts are remaining challenges.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A nearly full-screen mail window over a blue desktop. Folder tree occupies 220px at left; a compact message list sits above a spacious reading pane. Reply form anchors the message bottom. A blue taskbar closes the screen. Phones stack folder controls, list, and reading pane.

FORM: User-pinned period email client. Signature interaction: successful reply closes the envelope's unread state, reduces counts, and adds points.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
